# FPL-UK-Sales-Dashboard
Interactive Sales Dashboard built using Looker Studio

## Business Problem

The objective was to analyze historical UK sales data and identify revenue trends, product performance, customer behavior, and pricing insights.
note- here we used only tables for visualizing the number as per the requirenment from the authority, so any graphs.

## Tools Used

- Looker Studio
- Google sheet

## Dashboard Pages

- Revenue Dashboard
- Product Dashboard
- Customer Dashboard
- Price vs Quantity Analysis

## KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total COGS
- Total Profit
- Gross margin %
- Total Quantity
- Top Products
- Top Customers
- Total Customers
- Average Selling Price

## Key Insights

- Product A generated the highest revenue.
- Revenue peaked during specific periods.
- High-value customers contributed a significant share of total sales.
- Pricing influenced quantity sold across several products.
